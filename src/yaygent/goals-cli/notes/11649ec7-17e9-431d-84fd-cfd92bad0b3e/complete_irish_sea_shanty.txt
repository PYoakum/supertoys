THE EMERALD WAVE
An Original Irish Sea Shanty

SONG INFORMATION:
Title: The Emerald Wave
Style: Traditional Irish Sea Shanty
Duration: 3-4 minutes
Key: D Major
Tempo: 120 BPM (Moderate work pace)
Time: 4/4

CULTURAL AUTHENTICITY:
✓ Traditional Irish maritime themes
✓ Geographic references to Irish coastline
✓ Celtic cultural elements (shamrock, Celtic knots, selkies)
✓ Irish musical instruments (fiddle, bodhrán, tin whistle)
✓ Work song structure with call-and-response
✓ Storytelling tradition with mythological elements
✓ Community singing format

COMPLETE LYRICS WITH STRUCTURE:

[INTRO - Instrumental/Humming - 8 bars]

[CHORUS - All voices]
Heave away, me lads, heave away!
The emerald wave calls us today
From Galway Bay to the Celtic Sea
We'll sail until we're home and free
Heave away, me lads, heave away!

[VERSE 1 - Lead singer]
From the cliffs of Moher we set our sail
With the morning mist and a western gale
The fiddle plays and the bodhrán beats
As we haul the lines with calloused feet
The shamrock green upon our mast
Will guide us home when the storm has passed

[CHORUS - All voices]
Heave away, me lads, heave away!
The emerald wave calls us today
From Galway Bay to the Celtic Sea
We'll sail until we're home and free
Heave away, me lads, heave away!

[VERSE 2 - Lead singer]
Old Seamus tells of the selkie's song
That echoes through the night so long
While Brigid's blessing keeps us true
Through waters dark and skies of blue
The Celtic knots upon our rope
Bind our hearts with sailor's hope

[CHORUS - All voices]
Heave away, me lads, heave away!
The emerald wave calls us today
From Galway Bay to the Celtic Sea
We'll sail until we're home and free
Heave away, me lads, heave away!

[VERSE 3 - Lead singer]
When the lighthouse beam cuts through the foam
We'll know that we are sailing home
To Dingle Bay where the puffins cry
And the Guinness flows when the work is dry
So raise your voice and sing with me
Of Ireland's sons upon the sea

[FINAL CHORUS - All voices, with energy]
Heave away, me lads, heave away!
The emerald wave has brought us home today
From Galway Bay to the Celtic Sea
We've sailed until we're home and free
Heave away, me lads, we're home to stay!

[OUTRO - Instrumental fade with traditional Irish ending]

PERFORMANCE INSTRUCTIONS:
- Begin with strong, rhythmic introduction
- Chorus sections: All voices in unison, emphasize the "heave" for work rhythm
- Verses: Single voice or small group, storytelling style
- Build energy through each verse
- Final chorus with triumphant, homecoming feeling
- Traditional Irish instrumental breaks between sections

SHANTY CONVENTIONS FOLLOWED:
✓ Call-and-response structure
✓ Work rhythm suitable for hauling
✓ Repetitive, memorable chorus
✓ Narrative verses telling a story
✓ Strong rhythmic pulse throughout
✓ Easy to learn and sing along
✓ Appropriate length for work tasks

This original composition captures the spirit of traditional Irish sea shanties while incorporating authentic cultural elements and following established maritime musical conventions.
