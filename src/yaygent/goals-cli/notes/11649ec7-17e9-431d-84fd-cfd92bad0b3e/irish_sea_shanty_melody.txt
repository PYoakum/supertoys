The Emerald Wave - Melody and Musical Structure

TEMPO: Moderate (around 120 BPM) - typical work song pace
KEY: D Major (traditional Irish key, easy to sing)
TIME SIGNATURE: 4/4 (strong, steady rhythm for work)

MELODY STRUCTURE:

CHORUS MELODY (Call and Response Pattern):
"Heave away, me lads, heave away!" 
D - D - F# - G - A - G - F# - D
(Strong, ascending phrase for the work call)

"The emerald wave calls us today"
A - G - F# - E - D - E - F# - G
(Flowing, wave-like melodic line)

"From Galway Bay to the Celtic Sea"
B - A - G - F# - E - D - E - F#
(Descending, storytelling phrase)

"We'll sail until we're home and free"
G - A - B - A - G - F# - E - D
(Resolving, homeward-bound feeling)

"Heave away, me lads, heave away!"
D - D - F# - G - A - G - F# - D
(Repeat of opening call)

VERSE MELODY:
Lower register, more conversational
Follows natural speech rhythm of Irish storytelling
Uses pentatonic scale elements (D-E-F#-A-B) for Celtic flavor

MUSICAL CHARACTERISTICS:
- Strong downbeats for hauling rhythm
- Rising phrases on "heave away" for work energy
- Falling phrases on story sections for narrative flow
- Modal inflections (natural 7th) for Irish character
- Simple intervals (3rds, 4ths, 5ths) for group singing
- Repetitive chorus for easy participation

INSTRUMENTATION SUGGESTIONS:
- Fiddle for melody lead
- Bodhrán for rhythm
- Tin whistle for harmony
- Concertina for accompaniment
- Voices in unison for work sections

PERFORMANCE NOTES:
- Chorus sung by all hands
- Verses can be sung by lead singer
- Natural breathing spaces built into melody
- Strong rhythmic pulse throughout
- Duration: approximately 3 minutes with all verses
