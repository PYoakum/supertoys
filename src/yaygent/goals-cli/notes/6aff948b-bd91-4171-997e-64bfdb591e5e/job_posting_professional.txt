POSITION ANNOUNCEMENT
SOFTWARE ENGINEER - FULL STACK DEVELOPMENT

COMPANY OVERVIEW
We are a dynamic technology organization committed to delivering innovative software solutions that drive business excellence. Our company culture emphasizes professional growth, collaborative teamwork, and technical excellence in all endeavors.

POSITION SUMMARY
We are seeking a highly qualified Full Stack Software Engineer to join our development team. The successful candidate will be responsible for designing, developing, and maintaining both front-end and back-end applications using modern web technologies and industry best practices.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES
• Design and implement scalable web applications using React.js and Node.js frameworks
• Develop and maintain RESTful APIs and database architectures
• Collaborate with cross-functional teams including product management, design, and quality assurance
• Participate in code reviews and maintain high standards of code quality
• Troubleshoot, debug, and optimize application performance
• Stay current with emerging technologies and industry trends
• Contribute to technical documentation and knowledge sharing initiatives

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS
• Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or related technical field
• Minimum 3 years of professional experience in full stack development
• Proficiency in JavaScript, HTML5, CSS3, and modern web development frameworks
• Experience with React.js, Node.js, and database technologies (SQL/NoSQL)
• Strong understanding of version control systems (Git)
• Excellent problem-solving and analytical capabilities
• Strong written and verbal communication skills
• Ability to work effectively in collaborative team environments

PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS
• Experience with cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud)
• Knowledge of containerization technologies (Docker, Kubernetes)
• Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines and DevOps practices
• Experience with testing frameworks and methodologies

COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS
We offer a competitive compensation package commensurate with experience, including:
• Comprehensive health, dental, and vision insurance coverage
• Retirement savings plan with company matching
• Professional development opportunities and continuing education support
• Flexible work arrangements and work-life balance initiatives
• Performance-based bonuses and career advancement opportunities
