# Job Posting Content Extraction Guide

## Core Information Extraction
- **Job Title**: Extract exact title, identify key terms for SEO
- **Company Branding**: Logo placement, company name prominence
- **Location Details**: Full address, remote options, travel requirements
- **Compensation**: Salary, benefits, equity, bonuses

## Content Hierarchy for Newspaper Layout
- **Above the Fold**: Most critical information (title, company, salary)
- **Secondary Content**: Job description, requirements
- **Supporting Details**: Benefits, company culture, application process

## Typography and Formatting
- **Headlines**: H1 for job title, H2 for sections
- **Emphasis**: Bold for key requirements, italic for preferred skills
- **Lists**: Bullet points for responsibilities and requirements
- **Call-to-Action**: Prominent application button/link

## Content Prioritization
- **Must-Have Information**: Essential for job seekers
- **Nice-to-Have Details**: Additional context and benefits
- **Filler Content**: Company background, industry information

## Responsive Design Considerations
- **Mobile-First**: Key information accessible on small screens
- **Progressive Enhancement**: Additional details on larger screens
- **Touch-Friendly**: Buttons and links appropriately sized

## Accessibility Requirements
- **Alt Text**: For company logos and images
- **Semantic HTML**: Proper heading structure
- **Color Contrast**: Ensure readability
- **Screen Reader**: Logical content flow

## Interactive Features
- **Application Tracking**: Save/bookmark functionality
- **Social Sharing**: Share job posting capabilities
- **Contact Forms**: Direct inquiry options
- **Related Jobs**: Similar position suggestions
