POSITION: Senior Software Engineer - Full Stack Development

COMPANY OVERVIEW:
We are a dynamic technology organization committed to delivering innovative software solutions that drive business transformation. Our company culture emphasizes professional excellence, collaborative teamwork, and continuous learning opportunities.

POSITION SUMMARY:
We are seeking a highly qualified Senior Software Engineer to join our development team. The successful candidate will be responsible for designing, developing, and maintaining complex web applications using modern technologies and best practices.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES:
• Design and implement scalable web applications using React.js, Node.js, and related technologies
• Collaborate with cross-functional teams including product managers, designers, and quality assurance professionals
• Participate in code reviews and maintain high standards of code quality and documentation
• Contribute to architectural decisions and technical strategy discussions
• Mentor junior developers and provide technical guidance
• Ensure application performance, security, and scalability requirements are met
• Participate in agile development processes and sprint planning activities

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS:
• Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or related technical field
• Minimum 5 years of professional software development experience
• Demonstrated expertise in JavaScript, React.js, and Node.js
• Proficiency with database technologies (SQL and NoSQL)
• Experience with version control systems (Git) and collaborative development workflows
• Strong understanding of software development lifecycle and agile methodologies
• Excellent problem-solving and analytical thinking capabilities
• Outstanding written and verbal communication skills

PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS:
• Master's degree in Computer Science or related field
• Experience with cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud)
• Knowledge of containerization technologies (Docker, Kubernetes)
• Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines and DevOps practices
• Previous experience in a senior or lead developer role

COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS:
• Competitive salary commensurate with experience and qualifications
• Comprehensive health, dental, and vision insurance coverage
• Retirement savings plan with company matching contribution
• Professional development opportunities and continuing education support
• Flexible work arrangements and work-life balance initiatives
• Performance-based bonus structure
